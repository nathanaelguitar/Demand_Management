Scenario for Analysis
As a consultant for a national brand manufacturer, you have been assigned to determine the item unit price for the retailer of Brand #1. A private label has been gaining share within many of the manufacturer’s retail accounts and you have decided to price Brand #1 to potentially counter this problem. 
To assist in your deliberations, some historical data have become available from one of your key accounts that has no promotions in it. You have decided to use this data to determine the optimal price that will be charged to the retailer. You do not set the price to the consumer (i.e., the retail price) but only the price to the retail account. The retailer’s category manager, in conjunction with the retailer’s pricing department, sets the retail price. However, any changes in your price to the retailer will have a significant effect on the retail price. 
Within the accompanying Excel file, Table 1 provides you with the price per unit charged by the retailer to the consumer, the cases sold each week by the retailer, and the retailer’s case cost each week for your brand and for the private label. There are 24 units per case. Manufacturing cost data for the manufacturer are also given in the table. In addition to manufacturing cost, the only other variable cost incurred by the manufacturer is a selling cost of $10.00 per case. You do not know the manufacturing costs of the private label.
